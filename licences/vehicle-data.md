# Vehicle data — resale / redisplay permission

The vehicle full check shows vehicle-data providers' results to app users, which counts as
**resale** for every provider below (CarJam's terms: "Resale is passing CarJam data on to
anyone outside your business, whether you charge for it or give it away"). Before turning the
check on, keep the written permission for each provider you route to here.

| Provider | Permission needed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CarJam | A **Consumer resale** connection (chosen when applying for the API connection) | NOT YET — no account |
| AUCN | Written reseller / redisplay permission in the business agreement | NOT YET |
| MotorWeb | Written reseller / redisplay permission in the business agreement | NOT YET |
| Equifax CarHistory | Written reseller / redisplay permission in the business agreement | NOT YET |
| PPSR (B2G) | B2G terms allow showing search results / certificates to the searcher's customer | NOT YET |
| BusinessAPI.com.au | Their terms for passing PPSR results to end users | Check before use |

Save each signed agreement (PDF or email export) next to this file, named by provider.
