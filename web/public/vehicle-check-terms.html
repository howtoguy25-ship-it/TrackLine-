Vehicle full check — terms

Effective November 16, 2026

What you get

One purchase is one full check of one Australian vehicle (by number plate and state, or by VIN): vehicle details, registration status and expiry, written-off records, stolen records, finance owing (PPSR, with the official PPSR certificate where the provider supplies one) and recorded odometer history, plus recalls when the provider returns them. Each part shows who supplied it and the date the data is current to.

Where the information comes from

Every part of the report comes from a third-party vehicle-data provider or government register named on the report. TrackLine does not create or change that information and can't guarantee it is complete or error-free. Records can be missing, late or different between states. Always inspect the vehicle and get a mechanic's check before you buy. The report is not a substitute for an independent inspection or legal advice.

If something doesn't load

If one part of the report can't be loaded, it says "Couldn't load — tap to retry" and you can retry it without paying again. If the vehicle can't be found, or the main registers can't be reached at all, you are not charged twice: a free re-check is saved to your account for you to use later. Buying the same vehicle again within 24 hours just reopens the report you already have.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play and their refund policies apply. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

What we don't show

Reports never contain the names or addresses of owners or of anyone with a security interest in the vehicle. See our Privacy Policy for how the number plate or VIN you enter is used.

Availability

The full check is available in Australia only for now.
