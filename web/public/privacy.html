TrackLine

Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

TrackLine is a live navigation, driving-safety, and community hazard/emergency-vehicle alert app. This page describes, plainly and accurately, what data the app actually collects, how it's used, who else sees it, and the choices you have — written to match what's actually in the app's code, not generic boilerplate.

Information We Collect

Location

TrackLine uses your device's GPS to show your position on the map, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and center the radius used to show you nearby community alerts. Your exact location is sent to:

  • Google Maps — to draw the map, calculate routes/directions, and search for places (this is required for the app's core map functionality; it's Google's map, so your location goes to Google the same way it would in Google Maps itself).
  • Our Firebase database — only when you choose to report an alert (the alert's coordinates), or as part of a nearby-alert lookup. We don't log a continuous history of everywhere you drive. If you're signed in with a real account (not a guest), we also keep a single "last-known location" value, periodically overwritten as you move — not a trail or history of past locations — visible only to the app's administrator (see "Who Can See Your Data" below).

The traffic-light/speed-camera map layer queries OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service — only the visible map area (a bounding box), never your personal coordinates, is sent to it. If you use the NSW FuelCheck fuel-price feature, your approximate location is sent to the NSW Government's own FuelCheck service to find nearby stations and prices.

Camera — AI Vehicle Detection

If you turn on AI Vehicle Detection, the app uses your camera to detect and track nearby vehicles, estimate their speed and distance, and flag any with active strobing emergency-style lighting. It does not attempt to identify a vehicle's type (e.g. police/ambulance/fire truck) — only whether it's a car or a heavy vehicle (truck/bus), and whether it currently has emergency-style lights actively on. This runs entirely on your own device — video frames are analyzed in memory and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded anywhere.

As part of this, the app also estimates the location of a detected vehicle's number plate and attempts to read it — for real-time display only, so you can see it on screen while the vehicle is in frame. By default this reading happens entirely on-device (on-device text recognition), and no plate image, location, or recognized text is ever stored, logged, associated with your account, or sent to us or anyone else. If a cloud OCR provider (Plate Recognizer, platerecognizer.com) is connected on our end as a fallback, the small cropped plate-region image may instead be sent to that provider's own servers to be read — this is a server-side setting we control, not a per-user toggle, and the image is used only to return the plate text, not retained by us. Either way, a plate reading exists only in your device's memory for as long as that vehicle is in frame, and disappears the moment it leaves.

Microphone — EV Radar (mobile app)

On the mobile app, EV Radar listens for emergency-vehicle sirens using your microphone. Audio is analyzed on-device in short windows and then discarded immediately — it is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. A detected siren only posts your location as a community alert if you've explicitly turned on "auto-share" in Settings (off by default).

Account & sign-in information

Every session starts anonymously — you can use the map, navigation, and alerts without ever creating an account. If you choose to sign in, we store only:

Sign-in methodWhat's stored
Google / AppleYour display name and email address, as provided by Google/Apple.
Phone numberYour phone number and a password you set. The password itself is never stored by us or visible to us — it's handled entirely by our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication), the same way any password login works. We can't see or recover it, only reset it via a fresh SMS code.

We also record when an account first signed in and when it last signed in, and which method was used. That's it — no other profile data is collected.

Alerts you report

When you report a hazard, police, emergency vehicle, crash, speed camera, or traffic light, we store its type, location, and the time it was reported, linked to your account ID (not your name) so you can delete your own reports. Alerts expire and are automatically deleted on a schedule — 45 minutes to 24 hours depending on type — they are not kept indefinitely.

Vehicle history checks (REV Check)

If you run a REV Check, the number plate, state, and optional VIN you enter are sent to our vehicle-data provider (BusinessAPI.com.au's PPSR Searches API) to return real make/model/spec data and, if a VIN is provided, stolen/written-off/money-owing status. This lookup is performed server-side using our own provider account — we don't store the plate/VIN beyond what's needed to show you the result and, if you choose, save it to your own vehicle history log in your account.

Purchases & subscriptions

Some features (like REV Check) are paid, one-time or subscription purchases made through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details — Apple and Google handle billing directly and only tell us that a purchase succeeded, for which product, so we can unlock the feature on your account.

Advertising

TrackLine shows ads through Google AdMob to support the free tier of the app. AdMob may collect and use device/advertising identifiers and general usage data to serve and measure ads, per Google's own advertising and privacy policies. You can reset or limit your device's ad identifier, or opt out of personalized advertising, in your device's own privacy settings.

Diagnostics & crash reporting

We use Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and performance data (e.g. how long a screen took to load, or an error's stack trace) so we can find and fix real bugs. This data is technical, not tied to your name, and is used only to keep the app reliable.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide navigation services
  • Deliver speed camera and road alerts
  • Display maps and routing information
  • Run AI vehicle detection and emergency-lighting/siren alerts
  • Provide vehicle history (REV Check) results
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Detect and resolve technical issues
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Manage purchases and subscriptions
  • Comply with legal obligations

Who Can See Your Data

Community alerts (type, location, expiry) are visible to other signed-in users of the app — that's the point of a shared alert map. Your account's sign-in history (name, email or phone, method, first/last sign-in time) and last-known location are visible only to the app's administrator — never your password, and never to other regular users. Last-known location is only recorded for accounts signed in with a real identity (Google, Apple, or phone) — never for anonymous/guest sessions — and is refreshed periodically while you use the app, not continuously tracked in the background.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share limited information only with trusted service providers that help us operate the application (see "Third-Party Services We Use" below), when required by law, to protect our legal rights, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

Data Stored Only On Your Device

Your app preferences — alert radius, region-wide toggle, fixed-zone toggle, map theme, road thickness, and similar settings — are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, the alerts database, and REV Check/fuel-price server functions.
  • Google Maps, Places, and Directions — the map, search, and routing.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — community-mapped traffic-light and speed-camera locations.
  • NSW Government FuelCheck — live fuel prices, when you use that feature.
  • BusinessAPI.com.au (PPSR Searches API) — vehicle history/spec data for REV Check.
  • Plate Recognizer — optional cloud fallback for plate OCR, only when connected on our end (see "Camera — AI Vehicle Detection" above).
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchases and subscriptions.
  • Sentry — crash reporting and performance diagnostics.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and improve our application.

Community alerts are automatically deleted 45 minutes to 24 hours after being reported, depending on type. Saved REV Check/vehicle history entries and account sign-in info are kept for as long as your account exists, or until you ask us to delete them. Anonymous analytics and crash-diagnostic data may be retained for statistical purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw permissions, including location, camera, or microphone access, through your device settings

You can use TrackLine without ever signing in. To exercise any of these rights, or to have your account or alert data deleted, contact us below and we'll action it directly — there isn't a self-service delete button in the app yet.

Children's Privacy

TrackLine is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that information from a child has been collected, we will delete it as soon as reasonably possible.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers (listed above) may process information outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards are implemented to comply with applicable data protection laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective when the updated version is published within the application or made available through the App Store. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically — the effective date above is always kept current.
