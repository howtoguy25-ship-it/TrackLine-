# Store listing — additions for the vehicle full check

Paste into App Store Connect (Description / What's New) and Play Console (Full description)
when the check is switched on. Not before: the feature is hidden until then.

**Description paragraph**

> **Vehicle full check (Australia)** — before you buy a used car, get one report with the
> vehicle's details and VIN, registration status and expiry, written-off and stolen records,
> finance owing (PPSR, with the official certificate) and recorded odometer history. Each
> section says where the data came from and how current it is. One in-app purchase per vehicle.
> Always inspect the vehicle and get a mechanic's check.

**In-app purchase names**: Vehicle full check (A$19.99) · Vehicle full check (A$24.99 tier).

**App Privacy (App Store) / Data safety (Play)** — add: "Other user content: number plate or
VIN entered for a vehicle check — used for app functionality, linked to the user (saved in
My checks), not used for tracking." Purchases: "Purchase history — app functionality."

**Review notes**: "Menu → Vehicle check. A sample report is visible without paying. The check
uses licensed vehicle-data providers server-side; no owner personal information is shown."
